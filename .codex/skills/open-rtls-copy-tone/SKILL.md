# Open Location Stack Copy Tone

Use this skill when editing user-facing copy in `/Users/jillesvangurp/git/open-rtls/website`.

## Rules

1. Prefer direct, concrete language over broad marketing phrasing.
2. Name the actual product, technology, standard, or integration problem whenever possible.
3. Keep claims tied to what the repositories, docs, architecture, or current product direction can support.
4. Keep copy short. Cut clauses that repeat the same point.
5. Avoid stock rhetorical scaffolding such as `not just`, `not only`, `the point is not`, `that matters because`, `this matters because`, or similar contrast formulas.
6. Avoid vague phrases such as `ecosystem`, `shared foundation`, `operational layer`, `unified view`, or `platform` unless the page makes them specific immediately.
7. Do not collapse the scope to RTLS-only language when the actual scope is broader location technology, mapping, or interoperability work.
8. Give each page a clear job. Do not repeat the same positioning paragraph across homepage, product pages, FAQ, and news.

## Scope

1. Use this skill for `data/en/home.yaml`, product data files under `data/en/products/`, and editable content pages under `content/`.
2. Do not rewrite generated docs under `content/open-location-hub/docs/` unless the task is clearly limited to metadata polish.
3. Keep legal pages accurate and minimal. Favor correctness over stylistic cleanup there.

## Validation

1. Read the full page after editing and remove phrases that could fit any generic B2B software site.
2. Remove sentence starters that explain why the previous sentence matters instead of stating the point directly.
3. Check that links, standards references, release dates, and technical details still match the content they describe.
