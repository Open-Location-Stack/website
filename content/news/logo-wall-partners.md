We just added the first external company logos to the Open Location Stack website.

Initial outreach around the Open Location Stack initiative started a few weeks ago, and we have already received a lot of positive feedback from companies in our network.

We are very pleased to add the logos of [INDUTRAX](https://www.indutrax.com/en), [Sinfosy](https://sinfosy.com/en/), and [SynchronicIT](https://www.synchronicit.nl/) to the website. We look forward to working with them on Open Location Hub and the other Open Location Stack components.

## The first companies on the wall

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/Logo_INDUTRAX_aBrandofSMF_RGB_2025.webp" alt="INDUTRAX logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[INDUTRAX](https://www.indutrax.com/en) is an established smart factory software vendor with deep RTLS and identification expertise. On its own site, the company positions its software around configurable building blocks for location-aware manufacturing solutions, and it describes broad implementation experience across industrial roll-outs. INDUTRAX is also active in the omlox community and the INDUTRAX Application Environment integrates the omlox Hub standard interfaces and is fully compatible with the Open Location Hub.
</div>

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/SynchronicIT%20Logo%20only_blue_on_white_v1.4.svg" alt="SynchronicIT logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[SynchronicIT](https://www.synchronicit.nl/) is an established RTLS player. They provide UWB-based RTLS, OEM RTLS solutions, embedded wireless products, and omlox-compliant components. They were one of the early omlox members and are an active contributor to the omlox Core Zone specifications. This also means that they are fully compatible with the Open Location Hub.
</div>

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/Sinfosy_digital_UG%20ohne%20Rahmen_1.webp" alt="Sinfosy logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[Sinfosy](https://sinfosy.com/en/) focuses on practical Industry 4.0 solutions for manufacturing and logistics, and its SINTRA product line spans several tracking approaches instead of pushing one fixed hardware pattern. Their innovative camera-based and AI-supported tracking solution is something we hope to see supported via a connector for the Open Location Hub. 
</div>

We appreciate all three companies for backing Open Location Stack early.

## Next steps

If your company has its own tracking technology, indoors or outdoors, consider unlocking that location data through [Open Location Hub](/open-location-hub/). The hub already includes built-in support for configurable geofencing, optional Kalman-based normalization, and collision detection. That gives vendors and integrators a reusable path into interoperable APIs, downstream applications, and mixed-technology deployments.

If you are interested in learning more about Open Location Stack, adding your own Logo to the logo wall, or options for support and managed hosting , [contact us](mailto:open-rtls@tryformation.com).

We are looking forward to meeting with other omlox members at the omlox plugfest in June where we hope to be able to showcase some practical integrations between their tracking technologies and our FORMATION App via the Open Location Hub.
