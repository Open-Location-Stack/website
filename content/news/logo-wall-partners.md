Initial outreach around the Open Location Stack initiative started a few weeks ago at Logimat 2026. Since then we have received a lot of positive feedback from companies in our network. We just added the first external company logos to the Open Location Stack website.

We are very pleased to add the logos of [INDUTRAX](https://www.indutrax.com/en), [Sinfosy](https://sinfosy.com/en/), and [SynchronicIT](https://www.synchronicit.nl/) to the website. We look forward to working with them on Open Location Hub and the other Open Location Stack components. And we hope that more companies will follow.

## Companies

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/Logo_INDUTRAX_aBrandofSMF_RGB_2025.webp" alt="INDUTRAX logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[INDUTRAX](https://www.indutrax.com/en) is an established smart factory software vendor with deep RTLS and identification expertise. The company positions its software around configurable building blocks for location-aware manufacturing solutions, and has broad implementation experience across industrial roll-outs. INDUTRAX is also active in the omlox community and the INDUTRAX Application Environment integrates the omlox Hub standard interfaces supported by the Open Location Hub.
</div>

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/SynchronicIT%20Logo%20only_blue_on_white_v1.4.svg" alt="SynchronicIT logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[SynchronicIT](https://www.synchronicit.nl/) is a leading RTLS player that provides UWB-based RTLS, OEM RTLS solutions, embedded wireless products, and omlox-compliant components. They are one of the early omlox members and are an active contributor to the omlox Core Zone specifications. This also means that they are already compatible with the Open Location Hub, which implements all the relevant omlox hub interfaces.
</div>

<div class="company-blurb">
  <img src="https://assets.openlocationstack.com/images/openlocationstack/Sinfosy_digital_UG%20ohne%20Rahmen_1.webp" alt="Sinfosy logo" class="company-blurb__logo" />

[Sinfosy](https://sinfosy.com/en/) focuses on practical Industry 4.0 solutions for manufacturing and logistics, and supports sensor and tracking functionality via its  SINTRA product line. Their innovative camera-based and AI-supported tracking solution, which they demoed at the 2026 Logimat is something that we are particularly excited about adding support for via a connector for the Open Location Hub.
</div>

## Next

If your company has its own tracking technology, indoors or outdoors, consider unlocking that location data through [Open Location Hub](/open-location-hub/). The hub already includes built-in support for configurable geofencing, optional Kalman-based normalization, and collision detection.

If you want to learn more about Open Location Stack, add your logo to the wall, or discuss support and managed hosting, [contact us](mailto:open-rtls@tryformation.com).

We look forward to meeting other omlox members at the omlox Plugfest in June and hope to show practical integrations between their tracking technologies and the FORMATION app through Open Location Hub.
